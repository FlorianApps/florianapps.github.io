Connecting the remote control

  1. Open IntensivLotseSim on the Mac or iPad and enable the desired connection method in its connection settings.
  2. Open IntensivLotse → Remote control on the controlling iPhone or iPad.
  3. Select the same method: local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. For Wi-Fi, connect both devices to the same network; for Bluetooth, keep both devices nearby.
  4. Allow the requested Bluetooth or local network access, select the simulator and confirm its displayed PIN.

Connect inside the apps, not by pairing the simulator in the system Bluetooth device list. If it is missing, check both devices’ permissions and connection method. A device name and simulated case data are exchanged during the connection.

Sound

Enable sound in the app settings, check media volume and any connected headphones or AirPlay output, then play the test tone.
