Privacy policy

IntensivLotse · Beta version · Updated 29 October 2026

1. Controller and contact

Florian Bachmann
Email: florian.apps@icloud.com. Postal contact details are provided on the website home page.

2. App data

The apps are for education and simulated cases only. Do not enter identifiable real patient data. There is no separate developer-operated user account, advertising service or analytics service integrated into the current app version.

Preferences, favourites, shortcuts, topic order, preparation standards and saved simulated cases or reports, where supported by the app, are stored locally on your device. Device backups and files you save in cloud folders may be processed by your chosen backup or storage provider.

3. Remote control and file sharing

When you initiate a remote connection, the apps exchange connection identifiers, device names, simulated patient state, settings and commands using a local network or Bluetooth. Preparation standards are transferred only after confirmation. Exported files may contain your own notes or source information; check them before sharing. The developer does not receive this connection traffic or these files automatically.

4. iCloud status

This policy describes the current beta, which does not yet provide app-level iCloud synchronisation. Keeping the Xcode projects in iCloud Drive is independent of app data. This policy will be updated before a version with optional app synchronisation is distributed.

5. TestFlight and Apple diagnostics

Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information during TestFlight testing and makes data available to the developer. This may include your name and email address. Additional feedback, comments and screenshots you submit are also shared. This is separate from the absence of an in-app analytics service. See Apple: TestFlight & Privacy.

The developer uses beta data only to investigate faults and improve these apps. Downloaded reports are deleted when no longer needed for that purpose; necessary records may be retained for legal obligations. Apple determines retention within its own services.

6. Support requests

If you contact the support address, your email address, message and attachments are processed to respond to your request. The mailbox is hosted by Apple iCloud Mail. Requests are deleted when no longer needed, except where retention is required by law.

7. Legal basis and rights

Depending on the request, processing is based on providing the requested beta or support service (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) or the legitimate interest in answering enquiries, investigating errors and securing the service (Article 6(1)(f)). Statutory retention is based on Article 6(1)(c).

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability where applicable, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may complain to a supervisory authority, including the Saxon Data Protection and Transparency Commissioner. Contact the address above. Local app data is not held by the developer; deleting the app does not necessarily delete exports or device backups.

8. This website and external links

This static website uses GitHub Pages without added analytics, advertising scripts, external fonts or contact forms. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub Privacy Statement. Opening external references or email links involves the respective provider under its own privacy terms.

9. Changes

The policy will be revised if the app’s processing changes. The current published version and date apply.
